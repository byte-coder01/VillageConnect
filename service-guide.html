PUBLIC SERVICES

Before you apply

Use the responsible government or local service website for current forms, eligibility rules and application status. Before a visit, check which office handles your request and whether you need an appointment.

  1. Confirm the official service provider and its current requirements.
  2. Prepare only the documents the provider requests.
  3. Keep copies of your application and any reference number.
